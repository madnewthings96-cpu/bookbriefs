# BookBriefs Canva Ebook Template Refresh

## Objective

Transform the existing 22-page Canva design for *The Power of One More* into a polished, reusable Arabic ebook-summary system. Preserve the summary wording and current page order while improving visual consistency, readability, and reuse for future books.

## Visual Direction

- Use a warm ivory base, charcoal typography, muted sand dividers, and one adaptable accent color sampled from each book cover.
- Keep the Ta7leel logo, website, and page numbers as restrained footer branding.
- Prefer generous margins, consistent alignment, and purposeful whitespace over decorative density.
- Avoid unrelated quote screenshots and inconsistent image treatments.

## Typography

- Use one modern Arabic display style for the cover and section titles.
- Use one readable Arabic body style for paragraphs and lists.
- Standardize four levels: cover title, section title, chapter heading, and body/caption.
- Keep Arabic content right-aligned and verify mixed Arabic/English text for correct RTL/LTR flow.

## Reusable Page Types

1. Cover: book image, Arabic title, English title, author, and BookBriefs identity.
2. Introduction and author profile: concise title hierarchy, readable body, and consistently framed portrait.
3. Chapter opener: prominent chapter title with a clear starting point.
4. Chapter continuation: stable margins and body rhythm for longer summaries.
5. Evidence and action page: distinguish core teaching, why it matters, how to apply it, evidence, and practical takeaway.
6. Critical review and conclusion: visually separate evaluation from summary content.
7. Final CTA: one polished Ko-fi promotion with social and website references; no Ko-fi promotion on reading pages.

## Page-Level Rules

- Preserve the existing 22-page order and written content.
- Normalize headings, paragraph spacing, bullets, image scale, and footer placement across the full document.
- Use consistent labels for `التعاليم الأساسية`، `السبب (لماذا)`، `الطريقة (كيف)`، and practical takeaways.
- Remove the Ko-fi element from page 3 and retain one promotion on the final page.
- Retain useful book and author imagery but standardize its cropping, framing, and relationship to surrounding text.

## Validation

- Review every page at readable zoom and in grid view.
- Confirm there is no clipped or overflowing Arabic text.
- Confirm consistent margins, page numbering, footer placement, and accent usage.
- Confirm the final page contains the only Ko-fi promotion.
- Leave the Canva editor open on the completed design for user review.

## Scope Boundaries

- Do not rewrite, summarize, translate, or fact-check the book content.
- Do not change the page order or remove substantive text.
- Do not add unrelated brand elements or additional promotional pages.
